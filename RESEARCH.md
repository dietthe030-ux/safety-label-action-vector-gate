# Safety Label Action Vector Gate

Stage 1: translation can soften or alter who must act, severity, or conditional action in a public safety label. Validators compare only a sealed hazard/action vector. Actors: publisher, translator, distributor, validator, release workflow. Consequence: `RELEASEABLE`, `HOLD_TRANSLATION`, `UNSUPPORTED_LOCALE`. Unlike general translation fidelity projects, this is a fixed operational action gate controlling release. Stage 2 `SOURCE_SEALED -> TRANSLATION_SUBMITTED -> RELEASEABLE|HELD`; register/seal/submit/assess/correct plus gate views. Bind hazard, severity, actor, mandatory/prohibited actions, condition/time and evidence hashes. Publisher authorization, locale/size bounds, untrusted document isolation, fail-closed disagreement and idempotent replay. Tests cover negation, actor swap, severity downgrade, omission, stylistic change, injection, unavailable source and correction. Two short texts, one call, no payment; high feasibility. It does not certify product safety or legal compliance.
